Interactive guide

SP-1 Tape Looper 4.0

Gestures, takes, Bluetooth input and your music library.

Start & record

Connect your sound

USB-C or Bluetooth audio in

Select SP-1 as your computer or phone’s USB audio output, or pair it over Bluetooth. Both inputs can play together.

The headphone jack is an output. Bluetooth audio input is separate from the companion’s Bluetooth control connection.

Wake. Power off.

Hold FN · 1.5 s / 2.5 s

Hold FN about 1.5 seconds to wake. To power off, stop playback and hold FN about 2.5 seconds.

USB power can keep the charging gauge active.

Make your first loop

Arm → send sound → tap to finish

Tap an empty track to arm it. Start your audio to begin recording. Tap the same track again to finish the loop, then raise its fader.

Without a grid, input sound starts recording; with a grid, recording follows its punch-in. Tap PLAY while armed to cancel and pause.

Play. Stop. Restart.

Tap PLAY · hold to restart

Tap PLAY to pause or resume. Hold PLAY for about half a second, then release to restart.

Mix four tracks

F1–F4 = track levels

In track mode, raise a fader to bring its track forward; lower it to turn that track down.

Set the listening level

VOL− / VOL+

Use the two round buttons on the top edge to change master volume.

Mute. Restore.

Tap a loaded track

Tap a loaded track to mute it. Tap again to restore it.

Several track buttons can toggle their mutes together. Muting keeps the recorded audio.

Keep more than one take

TRACK + ROCKER DOWN = saved takes · TRACK + ROCKER UP = New

Hold a track and click ROCKER DOWN to cycle its recorded takes. ROCKER UP selects an empty New lane. Record normally there; your earlier takes stay saved. Each song and track has its own lane.

Close control pages and leave four-head mode before switching takes. Switch while playing or stopped. A New lane breathes when saved takes exist. Hold FN during playback to count takes.

Overdub the current take

Hold TRACK · release · tap TRACK

Hold a loaded track for 0.6 seconds to prepare overdub. Release, then tap it within 10 seconds to arm. Tap again to finish.

The earlier take stays saved in this track’s lane. TRACK + ROCKER DOWN finds it after the new recording is saved. Tap FN to cancel preparation.

Delete a take

Double-tap; hold the second press

Double-tap the track, holding the second press until deletion is acknowledged. This removes the selected take; other takes remain.

If the track is still saving, wait for saving to finish and try again. TRACK + ROCKER DOWN finds the remaining takes.

Find a song

Hold FN + tap T1–T4

Choose a bank with T1–T4. Repeat the same button to step through that bank’s four songs.

Use this from normal track mode. A longer FN + track hold opens a page instead.

Fixed or variable loops

Hold FN + PLAY · about 0.5 s, then release

Hold FN + PLAY for about half a second, then release both to switch Fixed / Variable.

Fixed rounds each take to a whole multiple of the base loop, keeping tracks aligned. Variable preserves each recorded length, so tracks can cycle independently. The side lights confirm your choice.

Adjust LED brightness

Hold FN + PLAY · 5 s

Keep FN + PLAY held through five seconds. The lights change while you hold.

This longer hold leaves loop length mode unchanged.

Tap a beat grid

Tap FN 4+ times

From normal track mode, tap FN at least four times in time. Start on the downbeat.

Fresh taps align recording to beats. The saved grid follows the loop; tape speed changes its tempo.

Clear the beat grid

Tap FN → press again and hold · ~1 s

Tap FN, then quickly press it again and hold about one second to clear the beat grid.

Solid track lights confirm. Recordings and the song’s beats-per-bar setting stay unchanged.

Round to the nearest whole BPM

Tap FN 4+ times → hold FN · ~1 s

Tap FN at least four times in time, then press and hold FN about one second to round the tapped tempo to the nearest whole BPM. For example: 119.8 to 120 BPM.

Near-whole tempos round within 0.4 BPM, from 50–200 BPM. A sweep confirms rounding; all-four flashes mean no change.

Read the track lights

Dark · dim · blinking · solid

Dark means empty; dim means muted. Recording is solid. Playback pulses with the grid, or when each loop wraps.

When stopped, an unmuted loaded track stays solid. Examples use 120 BPM and selected loop lengths. A refusal shrug can mean saving is still busy: wait for completion before deleting or recording again. Alternating T1+T3 / T2+T4 indicates a fault; stop testing and keep the device connected for diagnostics. In USB drive mode, T2 + T3 stay lit. After eject: T1 checks, T2 plans, T3 imports, T4 saves. Long imports can take several minutes; keep USB connected until Tape Looper returns. Repeated all-four flashes in drive mode indicate an error, not import progress.

Read the side lights

Playing: level · FN held: song

During playback, the meter fills from the bottom. Hold FN to see the song: solid = bank, blinking = position.

If bank and position share a light, that light blinks. With a running grid the song blink follows the beat.

Record from Bluetooth

Triple-tap FN and hold the last press until all four track lights start fading in and out, then release

Triple-tap FN and hold the last press until all four track lights start fading in and out, then release to enable Bluetooth audio discovery. Holding for 3 seconds turns Bluetooth off. Pair SP-1 from your phone or computer, select it as the audio output, then record normally.

The gesture also works during playback and USB audio. USB and Bluetooth input mix together. This release focuses on Bluetooth input; outgoing audio to speakers or keyboards is deferred.

Shape the tape

Change speed & pitch

Rocker: click / double-click / hold

Click for a speed step, double-click for a semitone, or hold to sweep. Pitch follows speed.

Pitch / speed mode: 0.25×–2×. Input recording locks speed; bounces allow speed moves.

Return to 1×

Hold FN + double-tap PLAY

Hold FN and tap PLAY twice. The tape returns to its normal speed.

Also clears Magnet gaps and Scissors cuts. Recordings, FX settings and the selected rocker mode stay unchanged.

Jump an octave

Hold FN + PLAY + rocker

With FN and PLAY held, click down for an octave lower or up for an octave higher.

No jump outside 0.25×–2×. Speed changes are locked during input recording; bounces allow them.

Shorten the loop window

Hold FN + rocker

Up halves the window. Down doubles it. Hold to keep stepping.

The chop stays inside your fader-set region.

Move the window

Hold FN + VOL− / VOL+

Step the chop earlier or later through your region.

With a page open, this same chord walks pages instead.

One window. Four faders.

FN + F1 / F2 / F3 / F4

F1 = start. F2 = end. F3 = position. F4 = size / zoom.

Edits start from the audible window. F4 down tightens; top restores the region set by F1–F3. Re-gripping FN preserves that region.

Reverse the window

FN + cross F1 past F2

Hold FN and move F1 (the start) past the end set by F2 to reverse the window.

Crossing edges during a beat repeat does not reverse it.

Whole loop or home

FN + PLAY + VOL− / VOL+

VOL− resets to the whole loop, forwards. VOL+ returns home while keeping chop size and direction.

VOL− also resets song/track trims, timing offsets and take length, and restores 4 beats per bar. Per-track reverse stays. During a repeat, VOL− / VOL+ move its window.

Reverse one track

FN + track first → tap PLAY

In track mode, hold FN + the track button first, then tap PLAY before its page opens. Repeat to reverse back.

Press order matters. FN + PLAY first, then the track, isolates instead.

Momentarily isolate

FN + PLAY first → track

In track mode, hold FN + PLAY first, then press the track to hear alone. Release the track to restore the mix.

Release the track to restore the previous mutes.

Enter four-head mode

Hold FN + triple-tap PLAY

Start with a loop playing in normal track mode. Hold FN and triple-tap PLAY. Repeat to exit.

Faders set head levels, track taps mute, and FN + faders set head positions.

Move heads. Change source.

FN + faders · hold a loaded track

In heads mode, FN + faders positions the heads. Hold another loaded track to make it the source.

Scissors / glue

Slot 7 · ROCKER DOWN marks, ROCKER DOWN cuts · ROCKER UP undoes

While playing, tap ROCKER DOWN at the start and again at the end of a section to skip it. With the rocker page open, hold the same TRACK for both marks to cut only that track. Bare ROCKER DOWN targets the loaded tracks.

Make up to eight cuts. ROCKER UP cancels a pending mark or undoes the last cut. Cuts remain when leaving the rocker page or slot. FN + double-tap PLAY restores the tape. Cuts are non-destructive session edits; changing songs, takes, recording or rebooting clears them. Use full forward loops, without trim, Heads, repeat or recording.

Choose a rocker mode

FN + both VOL · VOL− / VOL+ to choose

Hold FN and both volume buttons to open the rocker page. Release FN, then use VOL− / VOL+ to select a mode. Tap FN to close; the selected mode stays active.

1 Pitch / speed · 2 FF / RW · 3 Tape bending · 4 Battery drain · 5 Sandpaper · 6 Magnet erase · 7 Cut / glue · 8 Manual heads. Keep the rocker page open for per-track Magnet or Scissors gestures. Holding TRACK previews the normal track lights.

Wind

Slot 2 · hold ROCKER UP / ROCKER DOWN

Hold ROCKER UP to fast-forward or ROCKER DOWN to rewind. Release to let the tape settle back.

With the WIND page open, hold TRACK + ROCKER UP/DOWN: T1 0.5×, T2 1.5×, T3 3×, T4 4×. Release to return to playback speed. Double-tap TRACK to latch that winding speed for bare-rocker use, including after leaving the page.

Bender

Slot 3 · hold ROCKER UP / ROCKER DOWN

Hold ROCKER DOWN to drag and stretch the tape. Hold ROCKER UP for repeated pulling surges. Release either direction for a spring-like recovery.

Longer ROCKER DOWN holds stretch further. This changes playback; it does not rewrite your recordings.

Battery

Slot 4 · hold ROCKER DOWN / ROCKER UP

Hold ROCKER DOWN to increase battery-drain sag, wobble and stalls. Hold ROCKER UP to recover toward normal.

The wear stays when you release. FN + double-tap PLAY restores the tape.

Sandpaper

Slot 5 · hold ROCKER DOWN / ROCKER UP

Hold ROCKER DOWN to add roughness, breakup and tape drag. Hold ROCKER UP to reduce the wear.

The wear stays when you release. FN + double-tap PLAY restores the tape.

Magnet

Slot 6 · hold ROCKER DOWN to erase · ROCKER UP to undo

Hold ROCKER DOWN while playing to silence and mark the section passing under the head. Release to leave a gap without shortening the loop. Tap ROCKER UP to undo. With the rocker page open, hold TRACK to target only that track.

Unlike Scissors, Magnet keeps the loop’s length. FN + double-tap PLAY restores all Magnet gaps. Song changes and new recordings can clear the affected temporary gaps.

Manual tape head

Slot 8 · hold ROCKER DOWN + move faders

Hold ROCKER UP to make contact with the running tape. Hold ROCKER DOWN for four-head contact; move F1–F4 to position the heads. Release to lift contact and silence the tape.

The transport keeps moving while contact is lifted. Leave this rocker mode to return to ordinary playback. This is separate from latching Heads with FN + triple-tap PLAY.

Restore the tape

Hold FN + double-tap PLAY

Restore normal rocker tape behavior: clear Scissors cuts and Magnet changes, release wind and reset speed.

Solid track lights confirm. Recordings, FX settings and the selected rocker mode stay unchanged.

Trim a track’s length

Hold FN + PLAY · move that track’s fader

Hold FN and PLAY, then move F1–F4 to shorten or lengthen the matching track. Lower the fader to shorten; raise it to lengthen. Release both to keep the trim.

The first small move catches the current length without jumping. With a grid, lengths step through straight and dotted musical values. Other tracks keep playing; the original recording stays intact.

Slide the kept section

Hold PLAY · move the trimmed track’s fader

After trimming a track, hold PLAY and move its fader to slide the kept section through the recording. Its length stays the same. Raise the fader toward the start; lower it toward the end.

The first small move catches the current position. With a grid, movement follows sixteenth-note steps. Release PLAY to keep the position; moving the fader spends the PLAY press, so it does not pause or restart playback.

Effects

Open an effects page

Hold FN + a track button

Hold FN with a track button. Release both after the page opens. The page stays open.

T1–T4 opens Pages 1–4. Tap FN to close.

Move between pages

Page open · VOL− / VOL+

With any page open, press VOL− or VOL+ to move between Pages 1–8. Tap FN to close.

The same controls move between rocker modes when the rocker page is open.

Make the fader respond

Cross its stored value

Pickup prevents a parameter jump when you enter, leave or reset a page. Move through the stored value to take control.

Move through the stored value before a fader takes control. FN window edits respond to deliberate movement instead.

Filter

F1 · T1 bypass + reset damping

Move F1 for the filter. Center is neutral. Tap T1 to turn it off.

Hold T1 and move F1 for filter damping.

Chorus / Flanger

F2 · T2 type

Move F2 for depth. Tap T2 to switch Chorus / Flanger. Lower F2 to turn it off.

Hold T2 and move F2 for span. Hold FN and tap T2 at least twice in time to set its rate.

Distortion

F3 · T3 type

Tap T3 to cycle soft → hard → fold. F3 sets the amount. Lower F3 fully to turn it off.

Fader down gives clean audio; raising it restores the selected drive type.

Gate

F4 · T4 pattern

Move F4 for depth. Tap T4 to cycle Gallop / Halves / Slow gear. Slow gear fades into incoming notes. Lower F4 to turn it off.

Hold FN and tap T4 at least twice in time to set its rate.

Crush / Lo-fi / Radio

F1 · T1 type

Move F1 for depth. Tap T1 to cycle Crush / Lo-fi / Radio. Lower F1 to turn it off.

Ring mod

F2 · T2 off

Move F2 for ring modulation. Tap T2 to turn it off.

Auto-wah

F3 · T3 off + reset damping

Move F3 for auto-wah. Tap T3 to turn it off.

Hold T3 and move F3 for wah damping.

Echo

F4 · T4 division

Tap T4 to cycle 1/16 → dotted 1/16 → 1/8. F4 sets the amount; lower it fully to turn echo off.

Hold T4 and move F4 for echo feedback. Hold FN and tap T4 at least twice in time to set its rate. A plain track tap clears its tapped rate.

Phaser

F1 · T1 division

Tap T1 to cycle beat → half → quarter. F1 sets the amount. Lower F1 fully to turn it off.

Hold T1 and move F1 for phaser feedback. Hold FN and tap T1 at least twice in time to set its rate. A plain track tap clears its tapped rate.

Sweep

F2 · T2 division

Tap T2 to cycle beat → half → quarter. F2 sets the amount. Lower F2 fully to turn it off.

Hold FN and tap T2 at least twice in time to set its rate. A plain track tap clears its tapped rate.

Tremolo

F3 · T3 division

Tap T3 to cycle beat → half → quarter. F3 sets the amount. Lower F3 fully to turn it off.

Hold T3 and move F3 for tremolo shape. Hold T3 and move F4 for stereo spread. Hold FN and tap T3 at least twice in time to set its rate. A plain track tap clears its tapped rate.

Reverb / Freeze

F4 · T4 type

Move F4 for reverb. Tap T4 to freeze the current tail; tap again for Reverb. Lower F4 to turn it off.

Hold T4 and move F4 for reverb damping.

Tape drive

F1 · T1 off

Move F1 for tape drive. Tap T1 to turn it off.

Tone

F2 · T2 flat + compression off

Move F2 for tone. Hold T2 and move F2 for compression. Tap T2 to reset tone and compression.

Hold T2 and move F2 for compression.

Noise

F3 · T3 source

Move F3 for noise level. Tap T3 to cycle Cassette / Vinyl / Pink / Minidisc. Lower F3 to turn it off.

Wobble

F4 · T4 type

Move F4 for depth. Tap T4 to cycle Wow + flutter / Wow / Flutter. Lower F4 to turn it off.

Hold FN and tap T4 at least twice in time to set its rate. A plain track tap clears its tapped rate.

Tap or clear an FX rate

On the FX page: hold FN + tap TRACK 2+ times

Open the effect’s page. Hold FN and tap its TRACK button at least twice in time to set its rate.

To clear the tapped rate, hold FN + PLAY and tap its TRACK button. Rate controls: P1 T2/T4; P2 T4; P3 T1/T2/T3; P4 T4.

Hold a lane. Shape it.

Hold track + move fader

Hold a lane’s TRACK button and move its fader: P1 filter damping / chorus span; P2 wah damping / echo feedback; P3 phaser feedback / tremolo shape / reverb damping; P4 compression. For tremolo stereo spread, hold T3 and move F4.

Move before releasing the track button to avoid its tap action. After release, cross the main value again before changing it.

Shape the whole mix

F1 100 Hz · F2 350 Hz · F3 1.6 kHz · F4 4 kHz

Down cuts; up boosts. Center or a track-button tap returns that band to flat.

F1 and F4 are low/high shelves. EQ always affects the whole mix; lights show cut/boost amount.

Choose what gets FX

PLAY + VOL− / VOL+ / double-click

Hold PLAY and press VOL− for INPUT or VOL+ for TRACKS. Hold PLAY and double-click either volume button for BOTH.

Routes Pages 1–4; EQ always affects the whole mix. During a beat repeat, VOL− / VOL+ move its window instead.

Mute the input monitor

Hold PLAY + VOL− + VOL+

Hold PLAY and press both volume buttons to mute or restore live input. Works on any page.

Muted input is also silent in recordings and cannot arm a new input take. Unmute before recording.

Reset the effects

On an FX page: FN + T1 + T4

Hold FN and tap T1 + T4 together to reset all FX, flatten EQ and set routing to BOTH.

The four TRACK lights briefly turn solid. Effects, EQ, rates, types and routing return to defaults. Monitor mute stays as set.

While holding T3, move F3 down for a sharper peak or up for a softer peak.

Place & time

Stereo track placement

Faders 1–4 = tracks 1–4

Down moves left. Up moves right. Center keeps the original balance.

A mono take pans; a stereo take leans. Lights are dark at center and brighter farther to either side.

Center one or all tracks

Page 6: tap TRACK · FN + T1 + T4 for all

On Page 6, tap a track to center its placement. Hold FN and tap T1 + T4 together to center all four and reset FX.

The all-track shortcut also resets FX.

Move the downbeat

F1 = grid offset

Center is no offset. Up moves later; down moves earlier, by up to about half a beat.

Beat lights and future grid-aligned punches follow the offset.

Choose a take length

F2 = OFF / 1 / 2 / 4 / 8

Move F2 from bottom to top to select the preset length.

Variable mode counts bars and needs a grid. Fixed mode counts base loops and needs a first take.

Choose a length. Record a take.

Leave Page 7 → arm a track

On Page 7, choose a take length with F2. Tap FN to close the page, tap an empty track and let recording finish automatically.

The take stops automatically. Tap the recording track to finish earlier. Bounces do not use the preset.

Read the take countdown

Open Page 7 during a preset take

Up to four track lights show the units remaining. The last unit blinks.

Fixed mode counts base loops; Variable counts grid bars. Up to four lights show the units left.

Slide one track in time

Hold a track button + move its fader

On Page 7, hold a track button and move its fader. Up moves the track later; down moves it earlier. The edit is relative to the fader position when you held the button.

Nudge works without a grid. A recording track cannot be nudged. In heads mode, nudging the source moves all its heads.

Read the nudge lights

Amount while held · dim glow after

While a track is held, its light shows the nudge amount. After release, nudged tracks glow dim between beats.

The 1–2–3–4 chase shows the grid. Settings save per song after about two seconds.

Reset timing controls

Hold FN + PLAY · press VOL−

Hold FN + PLAY and press VOL− to reset the loop window, song/track trims and timing offsets. Take length returns to OFF; beats per bar returns to 4.

On Page 7, track taps clear that track’s trim first, then its nudge, then its page setting. FN + T1 + T4 resets FX instead.

Choose mono or stereo

Tap a track button on Page 8

Before recording, tap a track button to choose the format of its next take.

Solid light = mono. Blinking = stereo. Existing audio stays unchanged. Faders still control track levels.

Waiting or finishing on the grid

Half-beat flashes

An armed track flashes at half-beat rate while waiting for punch-in. The same quicker pulse marks a recording finishing its beat.

The recording continues until its aligned stop; it is not stuck.

Repeat & bounce

Make it shorter or longer

Hold PLAY → click ROCKER UP → resize

Hold PLAY and click the rocker up to catch a beat. ROCKER UP halves the repeat; ROCKER DOWN doubles it. Hold the rocker to keep stepping. Release PLAY to return to the song.

Release the rocker but keep PLAY held to repeat at the same length.

Repeat inside a chop

PLAY + rocker inside your chop

The repeat only uses your chopped audio. It cannot reach outside the audible cycle.

If a requested beat would fill the chop: ROCKER UP starts with a quarter of it; ROCKER DOWN with half, down to the minimum size.

Release: hold the tape

Release PLAY with FN released

On a gridded song, playback resumes from the repeat, with the tape shift rounded to the nearest bar.

A short stutter may leave the position unchanged. Without a grid, or during a bounce, release returns to the running tape position.

Repeat without a grid

Hold PLAY + rocker: ROCKER UP = 1/8 · ROCKER DOWN = 1/4

Without a grid, hold PLAY and click the rocker up for one eighth of the loop, or down for one quarter. Chop limits still apply.

On release, return to the running tape position. There is no bar-based tape hold.

Move the repeating window

Keep PLAY held + VOL− / VOL+

VOL− moves earlier. VOL+ moves later. Each press shifts by one current window length inside the audible cycle.

During a repeat these controls move its window, not FX routing. Set routing before catching the repeat.

Jump an octave in the repeat

Keep PLAY held + FN + rocker

Add FN and click the rocker. Down is an octave lower; up is an octave higher.

Bounce what you hear

Hold PLAY → briefly tap destination

Record the playing mix onto a spare track. Hold PLAY and tap the destination; tap it again to finish. This is a tape copy.

A loaded destination keeps its earlier take saved. Tape copy follows tape speed; grid, Fixed mode or chop can delay the stop to a cycle boundary.

Bounce a beat-repeat performance

Hold PLAY → click ROCKER UP → tap destination

Hold PLAY and click the rocker up to catch a repeat. Keep PLAY held and tap a spare track to start the bounce. Release PLAY to let the song flow back in; tap the destination again to finish.

Keep PLAY held to resize while recording. The destination’s earlier takes stay saved.

Make a sampler print

Hold PLAY → hold destination

Hold PLAY, then hold the destination track to record a sampler print. Release the track to finish.

Speed is baked for playback at 1×. The destination’s earlier takes stay saved.

Stop a bounce from a page

PLAY + destination track

Use PLAY + the destination track again to stop a bounce while a page is open.

Let the song flow back in

After starting the copy, release PLAY

The repeat ends and the tape copy continues from the running tape position. Tap the destination again to stop.

Sweep speed into a take

During a bounce: release PLAY → rocker

Use the rocker while a tape copy or sampler print is recording. Your speed moves become part of the performance.

Tape copy records speed moves relative to starting speed. Sampler print bakes the performance for playback at 1×.

Perform inside a sampler print

Keep the destination held · PLAY changes the rocker

Keep the destination held. With PLAY released, the rocker changes speed. With PLAY held, it controls beat repeat. Add FN for octave jumps.

Release the destination to end the print. Release PLAY too to end the repeat.
